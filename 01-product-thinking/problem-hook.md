# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, usage among our customer users will continue to decline, the largest enterprise customer may move to a leaner competitor, and RouteLogic will struggle to retain and attract customers that increasingly value speed and usability.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** People responsible for real-time fleet coordination are forced to work around RouteLogic using email, spreadsheets, and other tools to complete time-sensitive tasks, even though their company has already invested in RouteLogic as its logistics platform.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Product’s complexity is pushing operational users into workarounds, reducing adoption and putting major customer relationships and future market share at risk.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For the 95% of customers who rely on RouteLogic for day-to-day operations, we will simplify core workflows and remove unnecessary complexity so users can complete time-sensitive work faster, while preserving the advanced capabilities required by high-end enterprise customers.

## Cold-read your own hook
- **Is the business risk high enough to justify a new initiative, a high-stakes threat, or a minor inconvenience?:** The risk is high enough to justify a new initiative because declining adoption threatens customer retention, future growth, and RouteLogic’s competitive position.
- **Is the moment of misery a systemic problem or just an edge case?:** It is a systemic problem affecting 95% of day-to-day users. The remaining part who need high customizations are edge cases.
- **Does the value proposition actually remove the obstacle you identified, or is it a generic feature?:** Yes. It directly addresses the main obstacle by simplifying core workflows, while preserving specialized capabilities for customers who need them.

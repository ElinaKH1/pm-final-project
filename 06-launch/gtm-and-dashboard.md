# GTM Launch Plan, RouteLogic (B2B)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Feature | Driver Alert Notifications: Notify experienced delivery drivers when their assigned route changes, clearly show what changed and when, and provide direct access to the updated route. |
| Goal | Engagement |
| Launch tier | M, Targeted |

## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, Driver Alert Notifications introduces a new way for drivers to receive and respond to route changes. The launch should focus on helping drivers and dispatchers understand the feature, trust the alerts, and use RouteLogic instead of relying on calls or WhatsApp.
- **Target audience:** Primary: Delivery drivers who use RouteLogic throughout the day and may receive route changes while completing deliveries. Secondary: Dispatchers who make route changes and currently rely on calls or WhatsApp to ensure drivers see them.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Driver Alert Notifications changes how existing drivers and dispatchers receive and respond to route updates. Targeted communication and practical guidance are needed to build awareness and trust, but the feature does not require broad external promotion or a multi-channel campaign.

## Channels
1. **Owned: In-app announcement and onboarding prompt. Introduce Driver Alert Notifications inside RouteLogic and show drivers what the alert looks like and how to open the updated route.**
2. **Owned: Dispatcher-led launch briefing through existing shift huddles or operational calls. Dispatchers receive a short guide and demo to explain the new alert workflow, reinforce RouteLogic as the primary channel for route changes, and clarify when calls or WhatsApp should still be used as a fallback.**
3. **Owned: Targeted email and help article. Share the launch details, expected workflow, common questions, and support route with relevant customer contacts and users.**

## Enablement & Assets
In-app onboarding message; short driver walkthrough showing how to open an alert and access the updated route; one-page dispatcher guide; customer launch email; FAQ and troubleshooting article; support briefing covering delayed, duplicate, inaccurate, or wrong-driver alerts.

## Ownership, Budget & Timeline
- **Ownership & budget:** Product Manager owns the launch plan, messaging, coordination, and post-launch decision. Customer Success Lead prepares customer communications and enables dispatchers. Designer creates the in-app announcement, short walkthrough, and briefing guide. Engineering owns feature release, alert monitoring, and technical support. No additional launch budget is required.
- **Timeline:** Week 1: Finalize messaging, in-app guidance, dispatcher briefing, and support materials. Week 2: Brief Customer Success and dispatchers, confirm launch readiness, and release the feature. Weeks 3–4: Run the 14-day experiment, monitor adoption and guardrails, gather feedback, and decide whether to expand, iterate, or pause.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Percentage of eligible drivers who open at least one route-change alert during the first 14 days; percentage of alerts opened within five minutes; reduction in dispatcher calls or WhatsApp messages used to communicate route changes.
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Alerts are delivered, but drivers do not open them and dispatchers continue relying on calls or WhatsApp. This would suggest low trust, unclear messaging, or poor fit with the driver’s workflow.
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Expand if drivers use the alerts and manual workarounds decrease; iterate on messaging or the alert experience if delivery is reliable but engagement remains low; pause immediately if any alert reaches the wrong driver.

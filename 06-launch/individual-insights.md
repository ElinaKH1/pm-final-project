> _The personal reflection that closes your final project._

## Friction points

_The biggest technical or strategic challenge you faced while developing your product concept._

The hardest part was narrowing several valid user problems into one focused feature and defining a metric that directly reflected its value.

## Key learnings

_A few surprising discoveries or insights you gained from the course overall._

I learned to connect research, prioritize, scope, experiment, and launch decisions into one evidence-based story. I also learned that broader initiative metrics and feature-level metrics can serve different purposes.

## Aha! moment

_Your main "aha" moment during the project process._

My main realization was that successfully delivering an alert does not mean the feature delivers user value. The driver must see the alert in time for it to reduce the risk of following outdated route information.
